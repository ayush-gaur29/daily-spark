## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an editorial, reflective, and serene mobile atmosphere designed for mindful contemplation and focused inspiration. It strips away high-frequency notifications and visual clutter in favor of breathing room, deliberate typography, and high-clarity interaction zones.

The visual direction combines **Minimalism** with an **Editorial Touch**:
- **Purity & Space**: Generous vertical rhythm that treats white space as an active compositional tool, instilling an immediate sense of calm.
- **Intellectual Clarity**: The deliberate interplay between an authoritative, literary serif (*Newsreader*) for moments of reflection and a pristine geometric sans-serif (*Plus Jakarta Sans*) for functional UI establishes quiet confidence without relying on ostentatious luxury tropes.
- **Target Audience**: Discerning individuals seeking daily wisdom, grounded intellectual sparks, and mindful audio experiences free from gamified distraction.

## Colors

The palette is strictly cool, pure, and grounded. It deliberately excludes warm golds, brass, or dark-and-gold luxury schemes in favor of an architectural cobalt-and-ice harmony.

- **Primary (`#1E40AF`)**: A decisive royal/cobalt blue used for key actions, primary audio progress indicators, and prominent branding anchors.
- **Secondary (`#172554`)**: Deep midnight cobalt used for strong editorial contrast and dark accent cards.
- **Tertiary Surface (`#F0F7FF` / `#EFF6FF`)**: Cool ice blue acting as the primary surface tint for active tabs, highlight chips, and soft pill backgrounds.
- **Canvas (`#FAF8FF` / `#FFFFFF`)**: Pure cool white for peak crispness and legibility.
- **Text**: Charcoal slate (`#131B2E` / `#0F172A`) ensures deep contrast without the harshness of pitch black, complemented by muted slate (`#64748B`) for metadata.
